Use case: sprite sheet of four standing obstacle objects for a mobile game, drawn in the flat vector style of Image 1 (the approved concept frame: dull grey-beige office props #A8A39A / #8C887F, paper #EDEAE2, dark #2B2E34, no saturated colours).
Subject: FOUR separate objects arranged in a 2x2 grid with VERY WIDE empty gutters (at least 25% of the canvas width and height empty around each object), each about 20% of the canvas width and about 30% of its height, each standing UPRIGHT on a perfectly FLAT horizontal bottom edge (so it can sit on a cabinet), all belong to a boring rival bank branch: (1) a ticket-dispenser terminal: grey boxy kiosk with a small blank dark screen, a paper ticket strip sticking out of the slot, no buttons with digits; (2) a messy pile of beige paper forms with blank grey lines (no letters); (3) an old grey cash register with a blank small display, a drawer and rows of plain round keys with NO digits; (4) a chubby beige piggy bank with a coin slot on its back and grey cobwebs on it.
Constraints: flat solid pure magenta #FF00FF background everywhere else (no gradient, no floor line), the objects must not touch each other or the canvas edge, no shadows on the background, NO text, NO letters, NO digits, NO logos, no glow, crisp clean vector edges, strictly front view without perspective, soft light from the upper left, each object has 2-3 flat tones.
