Use case: concept key frame of a mobile playable ad, vertical 2:3, the first screen the viewer sees. A casual Flappy-Bird-style one-tap game screen, inside the phone screen only (no phone frame).
Subject: the inside of a dull grey bank branch of a rival bank, seen strictly from the side as a flat elevation. Dim grey ceiling strip along the top with two fluorescent lamps (one flickering), a grey wall with blank teller windows, blank empty posters, a water cooler and a sad ficus, grey floor tiles along the bottom. Along the wall, flat grey silhouettes of bored waiting people (two sitting, one standing, one old person with a cane) in a long queue. In the middle of the frame, left of centre, a glossy navy bank card (deep navy with a diagonal mint-to-violet glow gradient, small gold chip, soft glowing edge, EMPTY top-left corner) floats in the air, tilted slightly up, with a short trail of mint and violet sparkles behind it: it is the only bright, saturated object in the whole scene. On the right, the first obstacle pair with a wide gap exactly at the height of the card: from the ceiling hangs on two thin chains a dark electronic queue board with a blank screen, and a blank beige rounded plaque is attached just above its lower edge; from the floor rises a stack of two grey filing cabinets topped by a grey ticket-dispenser terminal.
HUD (flat, blank, no text): three small card icons top left (lives), a glass pill with a gold coin icon top right, a row of eight small dots with a tiny door icon at the end top centre, a wide blank rounded headline banner below them, and a blank rounded hint plaque with a small tapping hand above the card.
Composition: portrait 2:3, card at about 30% from the left and 45% from the top, obstacle at about 75% from the left, lots of calm grey space, the saturated card pops against the dull office.
Constraints: NO text, NO letters, NO digits, NO logos, no phone frame, no watermark, no perspective. Original designs.
