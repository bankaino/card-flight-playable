Use case: concept key frame of a mobile playable ad, vertical 2:3, the first screen. Image 1 is the approved first version of this frame: keep EXACTLY the same art style, the same grey bank branch interior (ceiling lamps, wall, posters, ficus, water cooler, waiting grey silhouettes, floor tiles), the same card design, the same obstacle objects and the same layout. Change ONLY the proportions, the hero must dominate:
1. The bank card is MUCH BIGGER: about 36% of the frame width (roughly 370 px wide in a 1024 px frame, 235 px tall), same design (deep navy, mint-to-violet glow gradient, gold chip, empty top-left corner, glowing edge), same slight upward tilt, with the sparkle trail scaled up with it. Keep it at about 30% from the left and 45% from the top.
2. The obstacle pair is SMALLER and slimmer: the hanging queue board and the filing-cabinet stack with the ticket terminal about 18% of the frame width (roughly 185 px), the gap between them stays tall and generous (card is clearly smaller than the gap), chains thinner.
3. The HUD is SMALLER and lighter: tiny card icons top left, a small glass coin pill top right, small progress dots and door icon top centre, a slimmer blank headline banner (about 60% of its current height), a smaller hint bubble with the tapping hand next to the card.
4. Background people, furniture and lamps scale down a little (about 85%) so the card is clearly the biggest object in the frame.
Constraints: NO text, NO letters, NO digits, NO logos, no phone frame, no watermark, no perspective. Original designs.
