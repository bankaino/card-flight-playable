Use case: sprite sheet of four hanging obstacle objects for a mobile game, drawn in the flat vector style of Image 1 (the approved concept frame: dull grey-beige office props #A8A39A / #8C887F, paper #EDEAE2, dark screens #2B2E34, no saturated colours).
Subject: FOUR separate objects arranged in a 2x2 grid with VERY WIDE empty gutters (at least 25% of the canvas width and height empty around each object), each about 22% of the canvas width and about 34% of its height, each hanging UPRIGHT: a small dark metal ring sits at the exact top centre of every object (no chain or rope beyond the ring), all objects belong to a boring rival bank branch: (1) an electronic queue-number board: dark rounded rectangle with a blank empty dark screen, two small grey buttons under it and a blank beige label strip; (2) a giant office rubber stamp hanging by its handle: grey wooden handle at the top, black base at the bottom with dull brown ink on its flat face (no letters); (3) a big grey metal percent-sign sculpture (two circles and a diagonal bar, a symbol only) hanging by a ring at the top; (4) a sad deflated beige balloon, wrinkled and drooping, hanging by its short knotted string from the ring.
Constraints: flat solid pure magenta #FF00FF background everywhere else (no gradient, no floor line), the objects must not touch each other or the canvas edge, no shadows on the background, NO text, NO letters, NO digits, NO logos, no glow, crisp clean vector edges, strictly front view without perspective, soft light from the upper left, each object has 2-3 flat tones.
