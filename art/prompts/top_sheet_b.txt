Use case: sprite sheet of four hanging obstacle objects for a mobile game, drawn in the flat vector style of Image 1 (the approved concept frame: dull grey-beige office props #A8A39A / #8C887F, paper #EDEAE2, dark #2B2E34, no saturated colours).
Subject: FOUR separate objects arranged in a 2x2 grid with VERY WIDE empty gutters (at least 25% of the canvas width and height empty around each object), each about 22% of the canvas width and about 34% of its height, each hanging UPRIGHT: a small dark metal ring sits at the exact top centre of every object (no chain or rope beyond the ring), all objects belong to a boring rival bank branch: (1) a big paper price tag with a punched hole, a blank beige label and a grey string loop at the top; (2) a long unrolled contract scroll: cream paper hanging down from a wooden rod, covered with many tiny wavy grey lines that are NOT letters, curled bottom end; (3) an old rotary telephone (grey-beige body, a handset on top, a round dial with NO numbers) hanging by a ring on its top, a short coiled cord loops up to the ring; (4) a rectangular beige 'back soon' sign board with a blank empty plate and a small round clock face with two hands and NO numerals.
Constraints: flat solid pure magenta #FF00FF background everywhere else (no gradient, no floor line), the objects must not touch each other or the canvas edge, no shadows on the background, NO text, NO letters, NO digits, NO logos, no glow, crisp clean vector edges, strictly front view without perspective, soft light from the upper left, each object has 2-3 flat tones.
